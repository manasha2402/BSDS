Project Update: Distributed Music Streaming Simulation 
  
1. Group Members 
This project is being done by Ananyaa Mylsamy and Manasha Sekar (both in the EST timezone).
 
2. Experiments 
We plan to run three experiments:
* Max Concurrent Streams Before Rebuffering: We gradually increase the number of concurrent streams on a single instance to find the exact point where chunk delivery latency exceeds 500 ms. This tells us the hard capacity limit of one instance before quality breaks down. 
* Adaptive Bitrate Switching Under Load: We compare a fixed high-quality stream against an adaptive mode that drops to a lower resolution when latency spikes. This is important because real streaming services like Spotify use adaptive bitrate to prioritise continuity over quality, and we want to measure exactly how much re-buffering it prevents.
* Horizontal Scaling: We test multiple ECS instances under 500, 1000, and 2000 concurrent streams. This lets us verify whether our system scales linearly and identify where bottlenecks appear beyond a single instance. 
  
These experiments cover the three main levers of streaming performance: single-instance limits, quality adaptation, and horizontal scale-out.
